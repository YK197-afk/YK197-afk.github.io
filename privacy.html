Privacy policy

ÉCHO: Little Voice · last updated November 26, 2026 · version française

ÉCHO asks for no account and does not collect any personal data itself: your progress stays on your phone. To keep the game free, two partner services process some data: Google AdMob for ads and RevenueCat for in-app purchases. This page explains what, why, and how you stay in control.

Who is responsible

NEO TECH, publisher of the game ÉCHO: Little Voice. Contact: support@neotechmali.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, Memories, fireflies, Talents, skins), your settings and the state of your purchases are saved only in the game's storage, on your device. We have no access to them. They are erased if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional videos that give you a reward and, only between levels, a limited number of full-screen ads. To serve and measure them and to fight fraud, Google may collect:

  • your IP address, from which Google infers an approximate location;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • technical information about the device and the app;
  • your interactions with ads, and diagnostic data.

Personalized ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent window appears on first launch: you can accept, refuse or choose in detail. On iPhone, the game also asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Without your consent, ads are not personalized.

Changing your mind. The “Ad choices” button on the Skins screen reopens the consent window when your region requires it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or refuse tracking in iPhone settings (Privacy & Security, Tracking).

The “No Ads” purchase removes the ads between levels. Rewarded videos are still offered, and only play if you choose to watch them.

Learn more: how Google uses data from partner apps.

In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Apple's App Store or by Google Play: we never see your payment details. To verify and restore your purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipts, an anonymous identifier created for your installation and technical information (such as device type, app version, store country and IP address). This data is not used for advertising.

Learn more: RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn them on, the game schedules a notification on your phone when your daily gift is ready (at most one a day, and nothing after a week without playing). These reminders are created and shown by your device itself: no data is sent for them. You can turn them off at any time in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

What the game does not do

No account, no name, no email address, no access to the microphone (the shout is a gesture on the screen), the camera, your photos or your contacts, no precise location. We do not sell any data.

Why this processing is allowed

Personalized ads and tracking rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalized ads, their measurement and fraud prevention rely on the legitimate interest of funding and protecting a free game. Verifying your purchases is necessary to give you what you bought.

How long

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall it. Data processed by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their own rules, described in their policies.

Outside the European Union

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing or receive it in a portable format, and you can object to its processing, by writing to support@neotechmali.com. Since we hold no data in your name, we will forward your request to the services concerned, for example to delete your history at RevenueCat. You can also file a complaint with your data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France (cnil.fr).

Children

ÉCHO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us and we will have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the change is mentioned in the game's release notes.
